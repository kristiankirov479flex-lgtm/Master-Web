Questions

Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend one, so you do not have to wait. Choose a free square on the chair board. Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free.

How long does a visit take?

A haircut takes about 40 minutes, and a haircut with a beard about an hour. The price list shows the time for each service.

Can I bring a photo of the cut I want?

Yes. Press a photo on the wall to book that cut, or show your own photo to your barber.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes. Children under 12 have their own price.

What if I need to cancel or I am late?

Please tell us as early as you can, so we can offer your time to someone else. If you are more than 10 minutes late, the service may need to be shortened.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, we accept cash and card.
