    06 Questions

    Good to know

    Do I need an appointment?

    We recommend one, so you do not have to wait. Build your visit above and print your ticket. Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free.

    How long does a visit take?

    A haircut takes about 40 minutes, and a haircut with a beard about an hour. Each tile shows its time, and your ticket adds them up.

    Can I bring a photo of the cut I want?

    Yes. Press a photo in the Work section to book that cut, or show your own photo to your barber.

    Do you cut children's hair?

    Yes. Children under 12 have their own price.

    What if I need to cancel or I am late?

    Please tell us as early as you can, so we can offer your time to someone else. If you are more than 10 minutes late, the service may need to be shortened.

    Can I pay by card?

    Yes, we accept cash and card.
