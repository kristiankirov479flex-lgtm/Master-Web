The roastery

Small batches, clear roasting

We roast 12 kg at a time and log every batch. That way we know what we send and can repeat it.

  1. 1 · Green coffeeWe buy straight from small farms and keep every lot separate.
  2. 2 · RoastingTuesday and Friday mornings. We watch the temperature the whole time.
  3. 3 · Rest and packTwo days of rest, then a bag with the roast date printed on it.

Every Saturday at 11:00: a free cupping at the roastery. Just come along.
