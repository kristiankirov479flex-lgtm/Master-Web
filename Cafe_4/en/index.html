About us

We opened the café eleven years ago in a house on a quiet Plovdiv street. We wanted a place where you can sit with a newspaper, a book or just a friend and nobody asks whether you will order again.

Every morning at six we start with the dough. We buy the coffee from a small roaster in the city, and the milk and eggs from a farm in the Rhodope mountains.

  • Cakes and pastries baked here
  • Oat and soya milk for €0.50 extra
  • Dogs are welcome on the terrace
