Your first visit

It takes about 40 minutes. You do not need to bring anything except an ID and old X-rays if you have them.

  1. 1

Common questions

Does it hurt?

We use a local anaesthetic and work slowly. If you want a break, raise your hand and we stop.

How often should I come for a check-up?

Twice a year. With gum problems or many cavities: every four months.

Do you treat children?

Yes, from three years old. The first visit is only to get to know us and we never treat a child by force.

How do I pay?

Cash or card after the treatment. For larger treatment we offer payment in instalments. Ask about insurance.
