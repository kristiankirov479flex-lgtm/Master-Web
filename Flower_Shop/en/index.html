Good to know

Can I order for today?

Yes, if you order before the cut-off time shown at the top of the page. Later orders are delivered the next day.

How much is delivery?

Delivery inside Plovdiv is 7 € on a chosen day and 12 € for express delivery within two hours. Picking up in the shop is free.

Can I choose the exact flowers and colours?

Yes. Write your wishes in the notes and we will confirm what is possible. Some flowers change with the season.

Can you add a card with my message?

Yes. Write the text in the form and we will add it to the bouquet.

What if the person is not at home?

Our courier will call the phone number you give. If nobody answers, we will call you.

Do you make wedding and event flowers?

Yes. Send a request with the date and a few ideas, and we will arrange a consultation.
