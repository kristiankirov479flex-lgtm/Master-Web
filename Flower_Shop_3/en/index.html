    Questions

    Good to know

    Can I pause or skip a delivery?

    Yes. Tell us by phone or message at least two days before the delivery and we skip it.

    Which flowers will I get?

    Whatever is best that week. The page shows this week's bouquet, and the calendar shows what is in season.

    Is delivery included?

    Delivery in Plovdiv is free. We also deliver nearby for a small fee; ask us.

    How do I pay?

    You pay on the first delivery, by cash or card. Then each delivery or each month, as you prefer.

    Can I give it as a gift?

    Yes. Tick “This is a gift”, tell us who it is for and we add a card with your message.
