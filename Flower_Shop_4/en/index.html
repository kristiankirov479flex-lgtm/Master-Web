      Questions

      Good to know

      How far ahead should I book?

      For weddings, three to six months ahead is comfortable, and peak weekends go first. For smaller events, a few weeks is usually fine.

      Is the consultation really free?

      Yes. It takes 45 minutes in the studio and does not oblige you to anything.

      Do you deliver and set up?

      Yes. Delivery and setup at your venue are part of the proposal. Travel outside Plovdiv is priced separately.

      How does the deposit work?

      A deposit (sample: 30%) secures your date and is deducted from the final price.

      Can I see flowers before I decide?

      In the consultation we show seasonal stems and sample arrangements.
