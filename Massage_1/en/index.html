Common questions

Will I be undressed?

You undress to your comfort. You are covered with a towel the whole time and only the part we work on is uncovered.

Does a deep tissue massage hurt?

You feel pressure, sometimes uncomfortable, but not pain. Say so and we ease off. It is normal to feel your muscles for a few days.

When should I not have a massage?

With a fever, an acute infection, recent surgery, thrombosis or cancer. If in doubt ask your doctor first.

How do I pay?

At the studio after the massage, by cash or card. You do not have to pay in advance.
