    Questions

    Good to know

    How long does a visit take?

    A gel polish manicure takes about 1 to 1.5 hours, and extensions 2 to 3 hours. The menu shows the time for each service.

    Can I bring a photo of the design I want?

    Yes. Pick any look in the lookbook, or bring or send your own photo, and we will tell you how close we can get.

    Is removing my old gel included?

    Removal is a separate service and is on the menu. That way the old gel comes off safely, without harming your nails.

    What if I need to cancel or I am late?

    Please tell us at least 24 hours ahead so we can offer your time to someone else. If you are more than 15 minutes late, the service may need to be shortened.

    Do I need to pay a deposit?

    For extensions and longer visits we may ask for a small deposit, which is deducted from the price.
