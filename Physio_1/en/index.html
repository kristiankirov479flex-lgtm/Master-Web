How we work

The aim is to get you back to what you do, and not to see you more often than needed.

  1. 1

    Assessment

    60 minutes of talking and examination. We tell you what happened and why it hurts.

  2. 2

    Plan

    You get a written plan: how many visits, how often and what it will cost.

  3. 3

    Therapy

    Manual work and exercises. At every visit we check whether you are improving.

  4. 4

    On your own

    You get a few simple exercises with pictures so the pain does not return.

Three things we do with everyone

Good physiotherapy is not just a massage. We combine hands-on work, exercise and explanation.

  • Hands-on therapy

    We work with our hands on muscles, joints and tendons to reduce pain and restore movement.

  • Exercise

    Everyone gets a programme they can actually do. We show it, fix the technique and make it harder when you are ready.

  • Understanding

    We explain what happened and what is normal. When you understand your pain, it tends to go away sooner.

See a doctor first if you have:

Physiotherapy is not for everything. These signs mean a doctor should see you first.

  • Sudden severe pain after a fall or a blow
  • Numbness or weakness in a leg or arm that is getting worse
  • Chest pain, shortness of breath or a fever together with the pain
  • Bladder or bowel problems together with lower back pain

Common questions

Do I need a referral?

No. You can come directly. If you have X-rays, an MRI or a hospital report, bring them.

Does therapy hurt?

Sometimes you feel pressure or a stretch, but it should not be unbearable. Say so and we ease off.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on the problem. We tell you after the first assessment. It is often between 4 and 10 visits.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in. For a shoulder or the lower back: a vest or shorts.

What if I do not improve?

After two or three visits we check progress. If nothing changes, we say so honestly and send you to a doctor for further tests.
