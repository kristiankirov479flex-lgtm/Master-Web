2. What is handled and why

Visiting the website

When you open the site, the hosting provider automatically records technical data: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser type. This is for security and for the site to work. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the “GDPR”). We do not use this data for advertising or profiling.

When you contact us

Demo mode. When you press “Book the table”, the details are saved only in your own browser (in “localStorage”) so the page can show you a confirmation and a reference number. Nothing is sent to us or to any server. [When the site is connected to a real booking system, replace this sentence: say that the booking is sent to our server, which database and provider hold it, who can see it, how long it is kept and the legal basis.] If you call or write to us, we handle what you give us: name, phone number, the number of guests, the time you choose, any allergies or occasion you mention and your notes. We keep it until your enquiry is dealt with and for [period, e.g. 12 months] after the last contact. Some data is kept longer where the law requires it (for example accounting records).

Choices remembered in your browser

The site can save choices on your device: the colour scheme you picked and, in demo mode, the bookings you send (so the confirmation, the free times and the demo back office page can show them). They stay with you (in “localStorage”) and are not sent to us. What you pick in “Reserve a table” and in the menu filter stays on the page only until you close or reload it, and is not saved. The language is set by the page address. We do not use cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising, and the site does not load fonts, scripts or images from other companies.

Map from OpenStreetMap

The map in the Contact section loads only if you press “Show the map”. Your browser then connects to OpenStreetMap servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. Legal basis: your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by pressing the button. Nothing is saved, so the map will ask again on your next visit. More about OpenStreetMap: OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

Links to other services

When you tap a phone number or an email address, your phone or email app opens and its own rules apply.
