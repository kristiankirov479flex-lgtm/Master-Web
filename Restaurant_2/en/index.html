    About us

    Ivan, who runs the tavern, had his father build this house more than seventy years ago. Today it has two rooms with wooden ceilings and a yard with a grapevine.

    We cook the family recipes: the gyuvech is baked in clay pots, the kavarma cooks for two hours and the meat for the grill comes from a butcher in town.

    • Clay pots baked in the oven
    • Vegetables from the market every morning
    • Delivery and pick-up until 21:30
