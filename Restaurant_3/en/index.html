Three halls

All halls have air conditioning, a sound system and their own kitchen. The hall is free from 40 guests.

  • The Vineyard hall

    up to 140 guests

    Our large hall with crystal chandeliers and a dance floor. Right for weddings and big celebrations.

  • The garden terrace

    up to 60 guests

    A covered terrace facing the garden, May to September. For christenings, engagements and summer birthdays.

  • The small room

    up to 40 guests

    A wood-panelled room with one long table. For family dinners and company lunches.

Menus per guest

The price is per guest and includes the hall, serving and service. We change the menu to your wishes.

  • Standard

    €28 per guest

    • A welcome salad and starters
    • A main course of your choice
    • Dessert
    • Soft drinks for 4 hours

  • Full

    €38 per guest

    • Three starters and two salads
    • A main course and sides
    • Dessert and coffee
    • Soft drinks and wine for 4 hours

  • Premium

    €52 per guest

    • A cold and hot starter buffet
    • Two main courses and sides
    • Cake, desserts and coffee
    • A full bar for 5 hours

We make separate dishes for vegetarians, vegans and allergies at no extra cost. Under 40 guests the hall costs €150.
