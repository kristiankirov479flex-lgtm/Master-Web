Our oven

The oven was built nine years ago and is lit every morning at ten. We bake on oak wood, so the crust is thin and lightly smoky.

We knead the dough with very little yeast and let it rest for two days. That keeps the pizza light.

  • 450 °Cis the oven
  • 90 secthe pizza bakes
