    Tyre guide

    Which tyres when

    The rule is simple: winter tyres below 7 °C, summer above. Here is the difference in a few words.

    • Summer

      above 7 °C

      Best grip and shortest braking on dry and wet roads in warm weather.

    • Winter

      below 7 °C

      Soft rubber and sipes that hold in cold, snow and slush.

    • All-season

      compromise

      Convenient if you drive little in town. Not the best in summer or winter.

    How much tread is enough?

    The legal minimum is 1.6 mm. Many drivers change summer tyres at 3 mm and winter tyres at 4 mm, because grip falls quickly. If in doubt, bring the car: the check is free.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Do I need to book?

    Not required. We take walk-ins when a bay is free, but with a booking you do not wait. The busiest times are late October and early April.

    How long does a swap take?

    About 45 minutes for four wheels. We show the expected time while you choose services.

    Do you store our tyres?

    Yes. The set is stacked and labelled with your plate and handed back at the next swap.

    How do I pay?

    On site, cash or card, when the work is done. The price you see here is an estimate; we confirm the final price before we start.

    Do you handle run-flat tyres and sensors?

    Yes, with the right machine. Mention it in the note so we reserve extra time.
