When to change tyres

Winter tyres go on when the temperature stays below 7 °C, usually in late October. Summer tyres come back in April.

The legal minimum tread is 1.6 mm. Many drivers replace summer tyres at 3 mm and winter tyres at 4 mm, because grip falls quickly.

How to read the size

The size is printed on the side of the tyre: width in millimetres, profile in percent and rim diameter in inches.

205 / 55 R 16 width 205 mm, profile 55%, rim 16″
