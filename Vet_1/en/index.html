What we have on site

You do not need to go elsewhere for tests. We do most things here, on the day of the visit.

  • An operating room for neutering, teeth and wounds

  • A lab: blood, urine and samples the same day

  • Ultrasound for abdomen, heart and pregnancy

Your first visit

You know what will happen and why. We do nothing without explaining.

  • A talk first

    We ask what you noticed and since when. Only then do we examine.

  • Consent before a procedure

    We explain what we will do, what it costs and the risks. We begin when you say yes.

  • Clean and sterile

    Single-use needles and gloves. The table is disinfected after every animal.

  • Cats and dogs apart

    Cats have their own waiting time, away from barking dogs. Ask when you book.

What to bring

  • The vaccination booklet or passport
  • Old test results, if you have any
  • A fresh urine or stool sample, if it is for that
  • A lead for the dog, a carrier for the cat
  • When it last ate and drank

Common questions

Do you see animals without an appointment?

Yes, if a vet is free. With an appointment you do not wait. In an emergency call us and we expect you.

How much is a first visit?

€25. If something more is needed (a test, a medicine) we tell you the price before we do it.

Are you open at night?

No. At night and on Sundays we send you to a 24-hour clinic whose phone number is at the top of the page.

Do you treat exotic animals?

Rabbits and guinea pigs: yes. Birds and reptiles: please call first and we will say whether we can help.
