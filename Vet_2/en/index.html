When a home visit is best

Not every animal should travel. This is when to call us.

  • Older animals

    Travel is tiring and painful for them. A check-up at home is gentler.

  • Nervous animals

    A practice is noisy and unfamiliar. At home the animal is calm and treatment is safer.

  • Several animals

    Three cats or two dogs? We come once and see them all.

  • Owners without a car

    Or with a small child, or when ill. You do not need to think about how to travel.

What we bring

Most things that are done in a practice we can do at your home.

  • A stethoscope, otoscope and thermometer
  • Vaccines in a cold bag
  • Blood-sample equipment, result the next day
  • Dressings, stitches and sterile instruments
  • Single-use gloves, needles and mats
  • Medicines for the most common cases

Common questions

Do you come in an emergency?

We are not a 24-hour service. During the day we try to come as fast as we can if you call. At night call the 24-hour clinic whose number is at the top of the page.

What does it cost if I do not know what it is?

The price of the exam and the travel is known beforehand. If something more is needed we tell you the price before we do it.

Are there things that cannot be done at home?

Yes: ultrasound, X-rays, surgery. Then we send you to a clinic and tell you whom to call.

Do you treat farm animals?

Not as a rule. Call and we will point you to a farm-animal vet in your area.
