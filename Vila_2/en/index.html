Good to know

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out until 11:00. If you arrive early, we will keep your bags.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is served from 08:30 and costs 8 € per person. Tell us about allergies when you book.

Do you accept children and pets?

Children are very welcome. Small dogs can stay in the Attic on request.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking at the door for up to three cars.

How do I pay, and what if my plans change?

You pay on arrival, by cash or card. Tell us at least three days ahead if you need to change or cancel, and there is no charge.

How do I get there?

We send exact directions when we confirm. The last part of the road is a short gravel track.
