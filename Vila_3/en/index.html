01 Our story

A cellar that learned to host.

The stone walls were laid by hand for a family press house. We kept the beams, the old wine press and the thick walls that stay cool in summer.

Then we added warm floors, soft beds and a long table, so that you can sleep where the wine used to rest.

Good to know

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out until 11:00. If you arrive early, we will keep your bags.

Can we taste the wine?

Yes. Tastings are on request, on the terrace, for guests and for visitors. Tell us when you book.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is served at the long table and costs 9 € per person. Tell us about allergies.

Do you accept children and pets?

Children are very welcome. Dogs are welcome in the Vineyard Cottage.

How do I pay, and what if my plans change?

You pay on arrival, by cash or card. Tell us at least five days ahead if you need to change or cancel, and there is no charge.

How do I get there?

We send exact directions when we confirm. The last kilometre is a gravel road between the vines.
