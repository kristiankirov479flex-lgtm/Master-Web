Good to know

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out until 11:00. If you arrive early, swim first: we will keep your bags.

Is the lake safe for swimming?

The water is calm and shallow near the dock. Children must be with an adult. There is no lifeguard.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is served on the terrace and costs 9 € per person. Tell us about allergies when you book.

Do you accept children and pets?

Children are very welcome. Dogs can stay in the Boathouse Studio on request.

How do I pay, and what if my plans change?

You pay on arrival, by cash or card. Tell us at least five days ahead if you need to change or cancel, and there is no charge.

Can I use the kayaks and the sauna?

Yes, both are free for guests. Ask at the house for an evening sauna slot.
