Common questions

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name and you receive all the files.

What do I need to provide?

Your texts and photos. If you don't have photos, I can arrange a photographer.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. After a short conversation you get a fixed price in writing.

Are there monthly fees?

A simple site can be hosted for free. You pay for the domain, usually around €10–20 a year, and for any extra services you choose.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Small changes are included for 30 days after launch, and I can add features or an editor so you can update the site yourself.
