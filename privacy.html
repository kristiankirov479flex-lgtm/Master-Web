2. What is handled and why

Visiting the website

When you open the site, the hosting provider automatically records technical data: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser type. When you contact me

When you send the request form (or write to me directly), I handle what you send: your name, your phone number or email address, the kind of business, the design you picked and your message. I use it to reply and, if we work together, to prepare an offer and a contract. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract and performing a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). I keep it until your enquiry is dealt with and for [period, e.g. 12 months] after the last contact. If we work together, some data is kept longer where the law requires it (for example accounting records).

Choice remembered in your browser

The site saves one choice on your device: the language you picked. It stays with you (in “localStorage”) and is not sent to me. It is needed for the feature you asked for. This site does not use cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising, and it does not load fonts or scripts from other companies.

Project previews and links

The previews in “Live examples” are loaded from this same website. Opening a project takes you to a separate website that has its own privacy notice. Please read it there.
